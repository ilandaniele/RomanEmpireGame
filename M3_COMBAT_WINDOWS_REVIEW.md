# M3.4 shared melee windows

Status: shared melee preparation/impact/cooldown implemented and tested; M3.4 remains open for death-to-objective/campaign wiring, any retained special/ranged action policy, animation/feedback and a fresh packaged-player check.

`FUnitCombatState` still owns one recovery clock. `TryBeginAttack` can reserve one impact after a valid wind-up; `Advance` consumes it exactly once before actor-side damage observers run. Wind-up is 25% of the recovery derived once from `1 / AttackSpeed`. Recovery begins when the action starts and is never refunded by a cancelled or missed hit. `AUnitBase` holds only the transient weak target/order revision or direct-attack kind needed to resolve that one impact. AI and direct control both call the same melee hit validator and `TakeCombatDamage`; direct control performs its pawn trace when the window opens, not on input. The validator checks live same-world enemy, melee range and vertical reach at impact. A moving target can escape before impact. An invalid/replaced order, Stop/Hold, possession switch or death cancels a pending hit. Committed damage callbacks can replace orders without a stale same-frame AI evaluation overwriting them.

`TakeCombatDamage` is the common health/morale entry for melee and existing ranged calls. It rejects self, same-faction and cross-world sources, but preserves null-source damage for environment/test paths. Direct melee also rejects an unpossessed caller before spending stamina. Existing armor/blocking reduction and death notifications remain centralized. The previous immediate on-screen AI "HIT" debug message was removed because it would now announce a hit before the window.
