# C-015A — Campaign encounter transaction contract

Date: 2026-10-05. Status: C-015A implemented and verified. This is the pure C-015 foundation, not runtime campaign battle application or the complete M5 journey.

## Contract

- New `Public/Campaign/CampaignEncounterState.h` / `World/CampaignEncounterState.cpp` define a frozen deployment and explicit local squad → strategic army/content/count mapping. They do not introduce a second live army or territory authority. Army and territory owners pass their canonical value states to the transaction; scratch copies are private and discarded on failure.
- Begin validates a distinct encounter handle, objective ID, attacking army/faction, currently owned source, hostile-owned adjacent target and all target defending armies. Empty targets and third-party forces fail closed until their policies are implemented; faction participation, diplomacy/war, catalog, gameplay phase and World validity remain runtime checks. Failed Begin cannot replace a pending deployment. Reads are detached.
- Each squad binds one army/content stack; multiple squads may share a stack. Invalid/duplicate squad handles, strategic GUID masquerading as tactical GUID, unknown army/content, nonpositive count and total over-deployment reject without changing bindings. Every deployed stack must be represented in full before result application.
- Tactical results now carry `EncounterID`. The pure evaluator accepts an explicit deployment ID or creates its own valid sandbox ID; its reset clears the ID. The current two-squad Rome/Carthage subsystem forwards an optional ID without changing existing bootstrap behavior. This does **not** authorize the sandbox as a campaign deployment.
- Apply validates the complete result before mutation: encounter/objective identity; unchanged deployed army identity/faction/location/composition, source/target ownership and graph; no newly arrived target forces; exact unique squad coverage/faction/initial quantity; nonnegative survivors/casualties with checked int64 arithmetic. Victory requires living attackers, no living defenders and the matching captured objective; defeat and draw cannot claim capture or inconsistent survivors/winner.
- On success, all casualty decrements are aggregated through explicit content bindings, empty armies retire, and only a victorious attacking army moves to the captured target. Target ownership changes in the same value transaction. Unrelated armies and settlement/production/graph state survive. Both values commit before the consumed encounter ID is recorded; no events, actor calls or world scans occur inside the transaction. Replays and ID reuse reject across later deployments until a new-campaign Reset.
- The consumed-ID ledger and local bindings are currently world-local values, **not** a versioned disk save or cross-world restore schema. Reinforcements without strategic bindings reject rather than mint inventory. Runtime reinforcement registration/receipts, transfer/session persistence and owner publication are not implemented here.
